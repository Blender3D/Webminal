Sponsors

Webminal has been free for everyone since 2011, with thanks to those who've helped keep it running.

Current sponsors

FOSS United: project grant (2026)

I'm really happy to announce that www.webminal.org has received a grant from FOSS United. It goes toward upgrading and running the servers, and toward ongoing maintenance, development (finally some room to try out new Webminal ideas :D), and support, keeping webminal.org a free resource for everyone. I truly appreciate their effort to support the FOSS ecosystem in India and across the world.

Anthropic: Claude for Open Source (2026)

I'm really happy and excited to announce that Anthropic generously provided six months of free Claude Max access, giving me room to explore what's next for Webminal, from new environments to development labs. Thank you, Anthropic for supporting Open Source development!
Past sponsors

Linode: cloud hosting (2022-2023)

Linode sponsored webminal.org's hosting servers from 2022 to 2023. Linode's support team is one of the best, and it has a great range of Linux distros. Thank you, Linode!
